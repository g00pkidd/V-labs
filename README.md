# V-labs
Students wants to play games truly unblocked games, so I created a project that helps that! meet V labs. A project that helps students play pc, n64, nes, and more games in school!
steps to play games. 
unzip file, then click on the folder, click on the math folder, click on math pages, then on math 23, and then click a game you like to play, then click on the google chrome icon and then enjoy the game!
Pasos para jugar:
Descomprime el archivo, abre la carpeta, entra en la carpeta «math», luego en «math pages» y después en «math 23»; elige el juego que quieras jugar, haz clic en el icono de Google Chrome y... ¡disfruta del juego!
